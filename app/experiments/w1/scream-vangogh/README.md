# Scream in van Gogh's Style

An SVG artwork that blends Edvard Munch's emotional intensity of "The Scream" with Vincent van Gogh's expressive painting style.

## Concept

This piece combines two of art history's most iconic emotional works:
- **Munch's influence**: The anguished figure with mouth open in a silent scream, hands to face, radiating anxiety
- **van Gogh's influence**: Swirling brushstrokes, vibrant color palette (oranges, golds, blues), and energetic lines that convey movement and emotion

The artwork uses animated SVG elements to add a living, trembling quality to the figure and swirling motions to the background.

## Elements

- **Background**: Deep blue night sky with orange and golden swirling waves
- **Figure**: Distressed human form with open mouth, rendered in van Gogh's expressive color palette
- **Distortion lines**: Radiating lines from the center to convey anxiety and emotional intensity
- **Brushstrokes**: Curved, flowing lines in the van Gogh style surrounding the figure
- **Stars**: Glowing circular shapes inspired by van Gogh's "Starry Night"
- **Foreground**: Bridge-like structure evoking the setting from Munch's original

## Animations

- **Tremor**: The figure shakes subtly to convey anguish
- **Wave sway**: The sky layers move gently
- **Pulse**: The distortion lines fade in and out
- **Twinkle**: Stars brighten and pulse
- **Wobble**: The foreground shifts slightly
